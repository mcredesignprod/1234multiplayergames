1234 Multiplayer – Games & Anti-Stress Games

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the 1234 Multiplayer app (the “Game”, “we”, “us”, “our”) handles, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the Game on Google Play (package name com.playergameinfinite.game).

We handle information in line with Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”), the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

1. Summary

  • No sign-up, no login, no account. You can play everything without giving us your name, email or phone number.
  • Your game data stays on your phone. Settings, player names, best scores and progress are saved only on your device.
  • We use Google Analytics for Firebase to learn which games and features people use, so we can improve the Game. This data does not include your name or anything you type.
  • We use Firebase Crashlytics to get a report when the Game crashes, so we can fix it (see section 4).
  • The Game shows ads from Google AdMob to keep it free. To show and measure ads, Google may use your device’s Advertising ID and other device information (see section 8).
  • We never sell your information.

2. Information we do NOT collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, photos, videos, audio recordings, contacts, files, precise (GPS) location, payment details, or any other information that directly identifies you.

The Game does not use your camera, microphone, contacts or location.

3. Information stored only on your device

To make the Game work, it saves the following on your device only:

  • Settings: language, music and sound on/off and volume, vibration, default bot level
  • Player names you type in (for example “Player 1”)
  • Best scores, the last game you played, and your place on the Home screen

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its app data in your phone’s settings.

4. Information collected by Google Analytics for Firebase

The Game uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service provided by Google LLC. When your device is connected to the internet, it collects:

App usage events
Opening the Game, screens viewed, session length, and Game events such as a game started or finished, the game played, play mode (vs bot, 2–4 players or relax), number of players, bot level and settings changed.
Device and app information
Device model, Android version, app version, screen size, language and time zone.
Identifiers
A Firebase app instance ID (a random ID for this install of the Game), the Android device ID that Android gives this app (sent with each event and crash report as device_id), and, where your device provides one, the Android Advertising ID.
Approximate location
Country or region, worked out by Google from your IP address. We do not receive your IP address or precise location.

Player names, scores you type, and anything that identifies you by name are never sent to analytics.

Events are stored on the device and sent in batches when an internet connection is available.

How long it is kept: Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically by Google.

Where it is processed: Google may process this data on servers outside India. Google protects it under its own security standards and data processing terms.

Google’s privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

The Game also uses Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google LLC. If the Game crashes, a report is sent the next time you open it with an internet connection. A crash report contains:

  • Technical details of the crash (which part of the Game’s code stopped working)
  • Device model, Android version, app version, and whether the Game was in front or in the background
  • The device ID described above, the screen and game being played, its play mode and bot level
  • A short list of the last things that happened in the Game before the crash, such as the events in the table above

Crash reports never contain player names or anything you type. They are kept by Google for up to 90 days.

5. Why we use this information

We use analytics information and crash reports only to:

  • Find and fix crashes and errors
  • Understand which games and features are used, so we can improve them
  • Find problems (for example, a game that people often quit early)
  • Make sure the Game works well on different phones and Android versions

We do not use analytics information to build marketing profiles, and we do not use it to contact you. Advertising data is handled by Google AdMob as described in section 8.

6. Internet use

All games work fully offline. The Game only uses the internet to:

  • Send analytics events and crash reports (section 4) when a connection is available
  • Load and show ads (section 8). Without internet, no ads are shown and the games still work.
  • Open this Privacy Policy and the Terms & Conditions pages from Settings

7. Sharing of information

  • We share analytics information and crash reports only with Google (Firebase), which processes them for us.
  • Advertising data is collected and used directly by Google AdMob and, where you allow it, its certified ad partners, under Google’s policies (section 8).
  • We may disclose information if required by Indian law, a court order or a government authority.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we do not give it to data brokers.

8. Advertising

The Game is free, and it shows ads from Google AdMob, an advertising service of Google LLC, to support its development.

Where ads appear:

  • A full-screen ad after a game, once you leave the score screen, and sometimes before a game starts
  • A small banner ad on menu screens (Home, Settings, Pause and Results)
  • Never while you are playing a game

Information Google AdMob may collect to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud:

Advertising ID
Your device’s Android Advertising ID, a resettable ID used for ads.
Device and app information
Device model, Android version, language, app version and network type.
Approximate location
Country or region, worked out from your IP address. Precise (GPS) location is not used.
Ad interactions
Which ads were shown, and whether you tapped or closed them.

Personalised and non-personalised ads: Ads may be personalised based on your interests, which Google works out from your Advertising ID and activity, or non-personalised, which are based only on general information such as the app and your approximate region.

Consent (Europe, UK and other regions where required): Before any ad data is used, the Game shows Google’s consent form, and you can accept or refuse personalised ads.

Your ad choices:

  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone’s settings: Settings → Google → Ads (the menu names can differ by phone).
  • Uninstall the Game to stop all ads and ad data collection.

We do not receive your personal information from Google AdMob, and we do not sell your information to advertisers.

How Google uses information from apps that show its ads: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Google’s advertising policies: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

9. Children

The Game is family-friendly and made for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal data that identifies a child, and no part of the Game asks for it.

Under the DPDP Act, anyone under 18 is a child. If you are under 18, please play with the permission and supervision of a parent or guardian. A parent or guardian can contact us at any time (section 12) to ask about, or delete, data linked to a child’s device.

Our ads are set to show only content suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly allow personalised ads for children. A parent or guardian can turn off personalised ads in the device’s settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

10. Security

We take reasonable security measures as required by Indian law. Analytics and ad data is encrypted while it is sent to Google, and only authorised people can access our Firebase project. No method of storing or sending data is completely secure, but we work to protect the information we handle.

11. Your rights and choices

Under the DPDP Act and other applicable law, you can:

  • Ask what information about you we process
  • Ask us to correct or delete it
  • Withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing consent stops future collection; it does not undo what was lawfully collected before.
  • Raise a grievance with us, and if you are not satisfied, with the Data Protection Board of India
  • Nominate a person to use these rights for you if you die or cannot use them yourself

Simple choices you have right now:

  • Stop all collection: uninstall the Game. Data saved on your device is deleted, and no more analytics or ad data is sent.
  • Limit ads: opt out of personalised ads in Android Settings → Google → Ads, or refuse personalised ads in the consent form where it is shown.
  • Clear local data: Android Settings → Apps → 1234 Multiplayer → Storage → Clear data.
  • Reset your Advertising ID: Android Settings → Google → Ads.

The Game has no account, so we cannot see your name. To make a request, email us from the address you want us to reply to. If possible, include your phone model and roughly when you played, so we can find the data.
